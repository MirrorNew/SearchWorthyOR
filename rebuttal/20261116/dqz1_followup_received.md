# Reviewer dqz1 follow-up

Source: reviewer reply supplied by the author in this chat on 2026-11-16. This file records the supplied text; it is not a new submission.

> The rebuttal strengthens the empirical evidence supporting the proposed approach. In particular, the additional same-backbone comparisons demonstrate improvements on C2, where applicable rules are omitted, and the full-model equivalence analysis provides stronger evidence that the gains reflect improved mathematical modelling rather than merely a correct final answer. The detailed failure analysis is also valuable in identifying limitations in information gathering, constraint binding, and model updates. The authors have additionally clarified the benchmark construction and comparison settings.
>
> However, two concerns remain. First, the evaluation still relies on constructed scenarios, and independent validation on real-world optimisation tasks remains future work. Second, despite the improvements, the full-model equivalence rate for SWAgent is only 55.33%, indicating that a substantial proportion of generated models still contain incorrect or missing constraints. These limitations are particularly relevant because reliable discovery and incorporation of external rules are the central contributions of the paper.
>
> Overall, I appreciate the authors' detailed response and the additional analysis. The rebuttal improves my confidence in the experimental findings, but does not fully resolve my concerns regarding external validity and the reliability of mathematical model updates.
